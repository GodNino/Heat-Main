Cambia · 3 preguntas nuevas

Questions

What restaurants ask us

Who can see our files?

Only managers, until a manager shares a folder or a single file with the bar, the kitchen, the floor or everyone. Files stay in your restaurant's private storage.

Do we need WhatsApp for the team anymore?

No. Each side has its own chat, managers can send a notice that reaches every phone, and messages clear after 30 days so old talk doesn't pile up.

How long can a dish video be?

Short is best: one or two minutes of the chef explaining the dish. It plays on the dish card, right after the photo.

Does my team need to download an app?

…the rest of the questions stay as they are.

The final call to action and the footer stay as they are. The “Everything around it” list loses nothing; chat and files move out of it and into their own sections above.
