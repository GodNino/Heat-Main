In one page

One app instead of the binder, the whiteboard and the group chat

A full-service restaurant runs on many loose pieces: a menu binder, a schedule app, a whiteboard for side work, printed BEOs, a group chat and a paper report at the end of the night. HEAT puts them in one place, on every phone, in English and Spanish. It was built inside a working restaurant and is in use today.

Run the shift

What managers use before, during and after service.

01

Manager dashboard

The first screen a manager sees: what needs them, then the day.

  • “Needs you” lists what is waiting, most urgent first, and changes with the time of day
  • The day on one timeline: side work, pre-shift, dinner, events and closing
  • People and hours today, covers and sales last night, against last week

02

Schedule

Build the week and publish it to every phone.

  • Front and back of house, each built by its own manager
  • Overtime and time off are flagged before publishing
  • Swaps and released shifts go only to people who can cover; a manager approves

03

Side work and sections

Hand out tonight's side work and sections in a few taps.

  • Opening, during-the-shift and closing jobs, by server and server assistant
  • Sections drawn on the restaurant's own floor map
  • Each person sees their jobs and section on their phone and ticks them off

04

Pre-shift

Tonight's board on the phone of everyone working.

  • The 86 list, what is running low, what to push and a note from the manager
  • Sent only to the people on today's schedule
  • Can be printed for the line-up

05

Private events (BEO)

The whole BEO, on every phone working the event.

  • Menu by course with counts, run of show, bar, setup and team
  • Guest allergies listed seat by seat, always with the “confirm with the kitchen” line
  • When the count or menu changes, the team sees what changed and confirms; a kitchen sheet and print are built in

06

Team chat

The floor, the kitchen, the bar and the managers, each with their own chat.

  • Direct messages, photos and voice notes
  • A manager's notice reaches every phone
  • Messages clear after 30 days, so it replaces the group chat without the clutter

07

End-of-shift report

How the night went, in two minutes.

  • Covers, sales, how service went, issues, what to order and notes for tomorrow
  • Allergy incidents and injuries are marked urgent and reach the owners right away
  • Feeds the dashboard the next morning

Train the team

What every server, bartender and cook studies on their phone.

08

Each person's home

What a server sees when they open HEAT.

  • Their next shift and their week, with swaps one tap away
  • Tonight's side work, section and events they work
  • What to study next and how far along they are

09

Menu cards

Every dish, cocktail and wine on a card.

  • Photo and a short video of the chef explaining the dish
  • Description, ingredients, price and allergens with their codes
  • Quizzes write and grade themselves; managers assign what to study and see who has done it

10

Allergy check

Answer the allergy question with the whole menu in front of you.

  • Pick the guest's allergies and see what not to offer and what has no recorded match
  • Based only on what the restaurant recorded on each card
  • It never calls a dish safe: the kitchen always confirms

11

AI tutor

A tutor that studies with each person before the shift.

  • Quizzes, explains and practises selling, using only the restaurant's own menu
  • When a card doesn't say, it tells them to ask the kitchen
  • Remembers what each person missed and starts there next time

Manage the restaurant

The rest of what a manager needs in one place.

12

Files

Specs, vendor price lists, BEOs and manuals, in folders.

  • Managers only by default; share a folder or a single file with the bar, kitchen or floor
  • Search finds words inside PDFs, Word and Excel
  • Word and Excel open inside HEAT

13

Team and invitations

Bring a new person in with one email.

  • Type their email and position; the invitation arrives in English or Spanish
  • They join with the right position from the first minute
  • Managers decide who can change the menu, the pre-shift, the team and the schedule

14

Employee of the month

Recognition the whole team takes part in.

  • Managers nominate; front and back of house each vote for their own
  • Results are announced in the app with the reasons people wrote
  • Past winners stay on record

15

Manager assistant

Ask the restaurant a question, get the answer from its own data.

  • Who is close to overtime, who hasn't studied, what the reports keep saying
  • Only sees what that manager is allowed to see
  • Changes nothing until the manager confirms

For operations

What it takes to run

QuestionAnswer
What does the team need?Their own phone. HEAT opens in the browser and can be added to the home screen, with notifications. Nothing to download from a store.
How long is setup?About a week: we load the menu, the team and the floor map, and train the managers in about an hour.
Who can change what?Each manager gets access only to what they run: menu, pre-shift, team, front-of-house schedule or kitchen schedule.
Is each location separate?Yes. Each location has its own menu, team and schedule; the owner can compare them.
Where is the data?In a private database in the United States. One restaurant can never read another's data, and files open only through short-lived links.
Does it connect to the POS?Not in this phase. Covers and sales come from the end-of-shift report.
LanguagesEnglish and Spanish on every screen; each person picks their own.
