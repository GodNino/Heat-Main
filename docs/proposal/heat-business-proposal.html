1 · Summary

What we're asking for

Full-service restaurants ask a lot of their teams. Servers learn a long menu and its allergens from a binder and memory. Side work, sections and the pre-shift live on a whiteboard and in group chats. The schedule and shift swaps go out by text. New people take weeks to be ready, and a mistake with an allergen is a risk to the guest and to the business.

HEAT puts all of that in one app on every phone, built inside a working restaurant. It is ready to use today; this proposal is not for something still to be built.

The request

Approve a 60-day pilot of HEAT at [1] location, starting [date], with clear goals agreed up front. At the end, we decide together whether to continue, using the results.

One app

Replaces the group chat, the menu binder, the whiteboard, the schedule app and the paper shift report.

Safer service

Allergens on every dish card, always with the codes and the “confirm with the kitchen” line. It never calls a dish safe.

Ready faster

New people study on their phone with an AI tutor that only uses our own menu.

3 · What HEAT does

Everything the shift needs, on one phone

For the team

  • Menu cards for every dish, cocktail and wine, with photo, a short video of the chef explaining it, ingredients and allergens.
  • AI tutor that quizzes, explains and practises selling, using only our menu. When a card doesn't say, it tells them to ask the kitchen.
  • Allergy check: pick the guest's allergies and see what not to offer and what to confirm.
  • My shift: their schedule, side work, section and tonight's pre-shift.
  • Team chat by side (floor, kitchen, bar), direct messages, photos and voice notes.
  • Every screen in English and Spanish; each person picks.

For managers

  • Schedule for front and back of house, with overtime and time off flagged before publishing; swaps go only to people who can cover.
  • Side work and sections on the restaurant's own floor map, sent in a few taps.
  • Pre-shift (86 list, dishes to push, notes) sent to who's working today.
  • Private events: the whole BEO on every phone working it; when it changes, the team sees what changed and confirms.
  • End-of-shift report and a dashboard; urgent reports reach owners right away.
  • Files: folders of PDFs, Word and Excel, managers only by default, searchable inside.
  • Assistant that answers questions from the restaurant's own data and changes nothing without confirmation.
  • Employee of the month voting, and email invitations for new people.

HEAT opens in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen, with notifications. Nothing to download from a store. Native iPhone and Android apps are on the roadmap (section 8).

6 · Security and data

The restaurant's data stays the restaurant's

  • Separated by restaurant. Every table is protected by row-level rules in the database, so one restaurant can never read another's data. An automated test checks this across the whole database on every change.
  • Access per person. Managers decide who can change the menu, the pre-shift, the team and each side of the schedule. Files are managers-only until shared.
  • Where it lives. Database and files on Supabase (PostgreSQL, United States, us-east-1); the app on Vercel. Private files open only through short-lived links.
  • Email goes out from heatplatform.io through Resend.
  • AI. The tutor and assistant use Anthropic's Claude through its business API, which does not use customer data to train its models. They only see the data the person using them is allowed to see.
  • Limits on email, AI and notifications protect against misuse and runaway costs.
  • If the restaurant leaves, we deliver an export of its menu, team and documents within [30] days on request, then delete its data.
  • Backups: daily database backups, kept for [7] days.

HEAT does not handle payments or card data and does not connect to the POS in this phase.
